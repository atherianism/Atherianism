About ATHERA

ATHERA is an independent philosophy founded by BDJS. It encourages critical thinking, ethical responsibility, compassion toward all living beings, respect for nature, and freedom of conscience.

Core Values

Reason

Seek truth through evidence and logic.

Compassion

Respect humans, animals, and nature.

Freedom

Protect freedom of thought and peaceful expression.

Integrity

Act honestly and responsibly.

Simplicity

Value a balanced life over excess.

Nature

Care for the natural world.

Atherian Oath

"I embrace reason over superstition, compassion over cruelty, simplicity over excess, and independence over attachment. I shall respect the freedom of others, live without imposing my beliefs, protect nature and animals, and uphold the principles of ATHERA with integrity and discipline."
